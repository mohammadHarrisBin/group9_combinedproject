================================================================================
                     VOLUME-WEIGHTED AVERAGE PRICE (VWAP)
================================================================================

1. WHAT IS VWAP?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VWAP stands for Volume-Weighted Average Price. Unlike a plain average price, 
VWAP accounts for the volume (quantity) of asset traded at each price point. 
Larger trades pull the average closer to their price, while smaller trades 
have less influence.


2. THE MATHEMATICAL FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
         Sum of (Price * Quantity) for all trades
VWAP = --------------------------------------------
               Sum of Quantity for all trades

In mathematical notation:
VWAP = (p1*q1 + p2*q2 + ... + pM*qM) / (q1 + q2 + ... + qM)

Where:
- p_t = Price of trade t
- q_t = Quantity (volume) of trade t
- M   = Total number of trades processed so far


3. WORKED EXAMPLE (BY HAND)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Suppose 3 trades occur in the market:
- Trade 1: Price = $100.00, Quantity = 2 BTC
- Trade 2: Price = $101.00, Quantity = 1 BTC
- Trade 3: Price = $102.00, Quantity = 1 BTC

A plain average of the prices would be:
(100.00 + 101.00 + 102.00) / 3 = $101.00

However, calculating VWAP:
1. Numerator   (Sum of Price * Qty) = (100*2) + (101*1) + (102*1)
                                   = 200 + 101 + 102
                                   = 403.00 USDT

2. Denominator (Sum of Qty)         = 2 + 1 + 1
                                   = 4 BTC

3. VWAP                             = 403.00 / 4
                                   = $100.75

Notice that VWAP ($100.75) is lower than the plain average ($101.00) because 
the largest trade occurred at the lower price of $100.00.


4. C IMPLEMENTATION STRATEGY (STREAMING ENGINE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In a streaming C program, you do NOT need to store past prices in an array or 
re-read files. Instead, you update two running total variables in constant O(1) 
time for every new trade:

- Variable 1: double sum_price_qty (accumulates p * q)
- Variable 2: double sum_qty       (accumulates q)

Algorithmic Steps per Trade:
1. Initialize both sums to 0.0 at startup.
2. For each incoming trade (price, qty):
   - sum_price_qty += (price * qty);
   - sum_qty += qty;
3. Current VWAP = sum_price_qty / sum_qty.


5. WHY VWAP IS IMPORTANT IN HFT & TRADING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Benchmark Indicator: Institutional traders use VWAP to evaluate whether they 
  bought or sold at a good price relative to overall market volume.
- Execution Efficiency: Buying below VWAP means getting a better price than the 
  volume-weighted market average.
- Low-Latency Efficiency: Because it only requires two floating-point additions 
  per trade, computing VWAP is extremely fast and ideal for high-frequency 
  trading engines built in C.
